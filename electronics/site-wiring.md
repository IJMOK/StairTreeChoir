# Site wiring

Generated by tools/gen_site_wiring.py from data/sites.json; don't edit.

**Defaults, not yet confirmed by Rob:** tree counts per site and every run length. Edit data/sites.json and run `make all`.

Each site: one enclosed Mean Well LRS PSU inside the site box (a DRiBOX Large), sized to the site; each tree on its own run from the site box, fused 5A on the DIN rail. The show player (Pi, sound card, PAM8610) has its own 3A fuse. Loads: 2.1A per tree (WLED's brightness limiter holds the pixels to 2A; full white unlimited would be 2.3A), 1.5A for the player. The PSU is the smallest that carries the site at no more than 85%.

The PSU's mains terminals sit under a printed cover; its mains lead comes in through a cord grip and plugs into an RCD-protected outdoor socket (or a weatherproof socket box on one).
