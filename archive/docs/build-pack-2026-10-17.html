Front, as seen from the lawn

Back, wiring channel in data order

Mirrored, as you'd machine it from the back. Lead enters at the bottom corner, runs the outline, then the mouth (wide, small, lip) and both eyes. Rout the channel about 15mm wide and 12mm deep so the spare lead between close pixels can coil in it.

How the mouth shapes map to xLights phonemes

Closedrest, MBP · lip line only Smalletc, FV, L, E · lip + small arc WideAI · lip + wide arc Round OO, U, WQ · small + wide arcs, no lip

12mm through-holes at 24mm minimum centres, outline inset 22mm from the edge. Test a hole on an offcut before cutting all fourteen.

02 · Pair kit

One box drives two trees

12V5VPixel dataAudio

Mains stays outside the box: the sealed supply plugs into an outdoor RCD socket and only 12V enters through a gland. Pixel power runs 12V straight to each tree output through its own 5A fuse. Every kit has an amp fitted so any of them can be the master.

05 · Order of work

Getting to December

  • Order pixels and pigtails this weekend. AliExpress usually takes two to three weeks.
  • Cut one prototype tree from the layout file and test-fit a pixel.
  • Build one pair kit and flash FPP onto the Zero. Check both outputs light a full tree.
  • Build the face model once in xLights, copy it six times, and sequence the first song.
  • Cut, oil and wire the remaining thirteen trees, then build the other six kits.
