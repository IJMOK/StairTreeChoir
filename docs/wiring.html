Stair Tree Choir · Site A, December 2026 · plan as of 28 Oct

How the power, sound and data go together

One mains plug feeds one power supply in the site box. Every tree gets its own fused 12V run and a plug-in connector. The trees get their show over Wi-Fi, so the only cable to a tree is power.

2 · Inside the site box

Seen from above with the lid open, to scale (1 square = 10mm). The DRiBOX narrows towards the floor and has ribs, so plan on about 290 × 190mm of flat floor. The parts sit on one printed plate. The plate isn't designed yet; measure the box and the PSU when they arrive.

3 · At each tree

The tree box sits on the tower of the left boot (the control boot), behind the tree. Power comes in through its floor; the pixel lead goes out through its floor, down into the tower, and up to the tree's own pigtail.
