Stair Tree Choir · Site A, December 2026 · plan as of 30 Oct (tree box: L base + clip-on cover)

How the power, sound and data go together

One mains plug feeds one power supply in the site box. Every tree gets its own fused 12V run and a plug-in connector. The trees get their show over Wi-Fi, so the only cable to a tree is power.

2 · Inside the site box

Seen from above with the lid open, to scale (1 square = 10mm). The DRiBOX narrows towards the floor and has ribs, so plan on about 290 × 190mm of flat floor. The parts sit on one printed plate. The plate isn't designed yet.

3 · At each tree

The tree box stands on four 10mm spacers on the back of the tree, low down on the node 1 side. An L base carries everything, including the three fittings firing down through its shelf, so nothing unplugs when the cover comes off. The cover hooks in at the top, swings in and takes one M3 screw at the bottom.
