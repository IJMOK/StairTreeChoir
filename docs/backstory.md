# The Stair Tree Choir: who they are

For anyone running a show night, and for the name tags. The six characters are in [show-night.md](show-night.md); everything here matches what they say and do in the skits.

## How the choir began (the 20-second version)

> For years they were a spiral staircase. Oak steps, going round and round, up and down, every single day. Every time someone ran up them, they creaked, and every time someone tiptoed down at night, they creaked a bit quieter. That's a lot of practice.
>
> When the staircase was taken out, the steps refused to be firewood. "We've been singing for years," said the top step. "Nobody noticed." So they became trees, grew some lights, and started a choir.
>
> Fourteen steps, fourteen trees. Six voices, because, as Holly says, "Not everyone needs to sing at once, Jingle."

## The six

Left to right as you face them. At a site with fewer trees, the first ones along are the ones you meet: Holly is always on the left.

### 1. Holly: the conductor

**Name tag:** *Holly · Conductor · "Places, everyone!"*

Holly was the **top step**. She spent years looking down on everyone and saw no reason to stop. She counts every song in, keeps everyone in time, and sighs a lot. She secretly loves it when the audience claps, and pretends not to.

- Catchphrase: "Places, everyone! Places!"
- Can't stand: Noel's long notes. Bramble's naps. Jingle's questions. (She loves them all really.)
- If you ask her anything: "Not now, we're about to start."

### 2. Ivy: the sidekick

**Name tag:** *Ivy · Assistant Conductor · "Especially you."*

Ivy was the step **just below Holly**, which is where she's been ever since: one step behind, agreeing. Holly and Ivy have been together longer than anyone can remember. Ivy is the one who notices when Bramble has dozed off, and is quietly the funniest tree in the choir.

- Catchphrase: "Especially you."
- Favourite job: whispering "He was asleep again."
- Secret: she knows all of Holly's words before Holly says them.

### 3. Noel: the tenor

**Name tag:** *Noel · Tenor (he'll tell you) · "Miiiiiii!"*

Noel was the **creaky step in the middle**, the one you could hear from every room. He has always thought of it as singing. He warms up before every show, holds every last note for far too long, and has been told "THANK you, Noel" more times than any tree in history.

- Catchphrase: "Mi mi mi mi miiiiiii!"
- Best moment: the end of "Goodniiiiiight!"
- Ambition: a solo. Holly says no.

### 4. Jingle: the excitable one

**Name tag:** *Jingle · Enthusiast · "Can I? Can I?"*

Jingle was the step that **squeaked when children ran up the stairs**, so he's been excited his whole life. He talks fast, asks to sing every song, and still can't believe he gets to be in a choir. Every single show is the best show ever.

- Catchphrase: "Ooh, ooh, can I sing the next one?"
- Favourite word: "Again!"
- Covers for Bramble on the fifth day of Christmas, every time, with Pip.

### 5. Bramble: the sleepy one

**Name tag:** *Bramble · Bass, mostly asleep · "I was resting my eyes."*

Bramble was the **bottom step**. For years everyone sat on him to put their boots on, so he's very, very tired. He means to stay awake, honestly, but one gentle song and he's off. When he's asleep his eyes close and his lights go slow and blue, breathing in and out. Watch for it.

- Catchphrase: "I was resting my eyes."
- Has slept through: Little Drummer Boy (the "pa rum pum pum pum" is a lullaby to him) and the whole of the Twelve Days of Christmas, including his own verse.
- Wakes up to: Holly shouting "Bramble!" He always answers "Present!"

### 6. Pip: the joker

**Name tag:** *Pip · Smallest voice · Terrible jokes*

Pip was the **little step at the turn** of the spiral, the one everyone forgot was there until they tripped on it. So Pip makes sure nobody forgets now: big jokes from the smallest voice. The jokes are awful and Pip knows it, which is the point.

- Catchphrase: "What do you call a tree that's afraid of Christmas? A fir-raidy cat!"
- Wants everyone to come back and bring their grandma (and their grandma's friends).
- Last word of the night: "Don't let the reindeer bite!"

## If someone asks

| They ask | You say |
|---|---|
| Are they real wood? | Yes, solid oak: they were the steps of a spiral staircase. |
| How do they sing? | Each tree has 92 lights and its own little computer on the back. A player in the box sends every tree its lights and plays the voices through the speaker. |
| Who's who? | Holly's on the left; then Ivy, Noel, Jingle, Bramble, Pip. |
| Why is that one blue? | That's Bramble. He's asleep. Holly will wake him up in a minute. |
| When's the next show? | The trees announce it every quarter of an hour. Shows are at 5, 6, 7 and 8. |
| Can I touch them? | Please don't: they're plugged in, and Bramble's a light sleeper. |
