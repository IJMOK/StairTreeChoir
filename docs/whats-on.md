## A normal night

| When | What's happening |
|---|---|
| 4pm | Lights on. Between shows the trees muck about: waves, winks, games of catch, and Bramble nodding off. Every quarter of an hour they tell you when the next show is. |
| 5pm, 6pm, 7pm and 8pm | **Shows**, about 11-14 minutes each: four songs, plus jokes and chat in between. |
| 9pm | **Goodnight**: the choir says goodnight and settles down. |
| after 9pm | The trees doze, yawn and giggle quietly, with the lights turned down low. Lights out by 11:30pm. |

The trees also wake up with us in the mornings: yawning, stretching and watching everyone go off to work and school.

## What's in each show

Three different shows, so if you stay for more than one you'll see something new.

| Show | Songs |
|---|---|
| The 5 and 8 o'clock show | Rockin' Around the Christmas Tree, Dominick the Donkey, Little Drummer Boy, We Wish You a Merry Christmas |
| The 6 o'clock show | Feliz Navidad, Frosty the Snowman, O Come All Ye Faithful, Rockin' Around the Christmas Tree |
| The 7 o'clock show | We Wish You a Merry Christmas, Twelve Days of Christmas, The Virgin Mary Had a Baby Boy, Dominick the Donkey |

## Special nights

| Date | What's different |
|---|---|
| Saturdays 5, 12, 19 and 26 December | At 5pm a short taster (two songs), then at 6pm the **Saturday Special**: every Christmas song we know in one go (about 28 minutes). The other shows as normal. |
| Wednesday 23 December: **Village Night** | The big one. At 6pm the **Village Special** with every song; the 5pm and 7pm shows have auditions for new voices from the crowd. |
| Thursday 24 December: **Christmas Eve** | Santa phones in during every show. At 9pm the choir falls asleep one by one so he can come, and they stay asleep all night. |
| Thursday 31 December: **New Year's Eve** | Shows at 5pm, 6pm, 7pm, 8pm and 10pm, a late show at 11:15pm (Bramble swears he'll stay up), then the **midnight countdown** and Auld Lang Syne. Lights out by 12:30am. |
| Friday 1 January: **New Year's Day** | New Year shows every hour from 3pm to 7pm: Happy New Year, Here Comes the Sun, Walking on Sunshine, What a Wonderful World, Auld Lang Syne. Then the choir says goodbye for the year at 7:30pm. |

## Meet the choir

Left to right: **Holly** (the conductor, bossy), **Ivy** (agrees with Holly), **Noel** (the tenor, holds every note too long), **Jingle** (very excited), **Bramble** (very sleepy) and **Pip** (the smallest, terrible jokes). More about them: [backstory.md](backstory.md).
